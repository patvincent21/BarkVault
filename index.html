How barkvault plays

Players move through each case by comparing facts instead of guessing blindly.

01

Inspect evidence

Players review photos, item logs, fingerprints, and scene notes to understand what really happened.

02

Break alibis

Witness timelines and suspect statements can be cross-checked against police and medical reports.

03

Make the accusation

When the contradictions line up, the player identifies the culprit and explains the reasoning.

Core investigation files

Every case in barkvault is built from documents players must connect on their own.

Evidence board

Physical clues and recovered items establish what is known and what has been planted.

Police report

Incident summaries, officer notes, and timestamps create the baseline narrative.

Medical report

Cause of injury, time-of-death estimates, and lab results expose impossible stories.

Suspect alibis

Each alibi sounds credible until one detail conflicts with the documented evidence.
